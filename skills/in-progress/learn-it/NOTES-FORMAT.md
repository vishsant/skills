# NOTES.md format

A scratchpad for how this user wants to be taught. Free-form, appended to whenever they volunteer a preference or you notice one that held.

Not for what they learned. That is `MAP.md`.

```markdown
# Notes

## Preferences

- Prefers Socratic questioning over narration, even when it is slower.
- Wants the analogy before the formalism, never after.
- Hates being asked to "guess" without enough grounding to reason from.

## Working notes

- Low energy on weeknights; keep sessions to one or two nodes.
- Reads the lesson files in Obsidian, so mermaid and LaTeX both render.
```

Record a preference when the user states one, or when a correction they made once would apply again. Do not record one-off reactions as standing preferences.
